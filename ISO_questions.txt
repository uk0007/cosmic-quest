This 50-question mock bank is tailored to the Class 6 International Science Olympiad (ISO / NSO) syllabus, structured into Logical Reasoning, Core Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), and the high-weightage Achievers Section (HOTS).

Section 1: Logical Reasoning (Questions 1–10)
1 Mark Each

1. Find the missing term in the given series:

4, 9, 19, 39, 79, ___?

(A) 119

(B) 139

(C) 159

(D) 169

Answer: (C) 159.

Explanation: The pattern is $(n \times 2) + 1$.

$(4 \times 2) + 1 = 9$;

$(9 \times 2) + 1 = 19$;

$(19 \times 2) + 1 = 39$;

$(39 \times 2) + 1 = 79$;

$(79 \times 2) + 1 = 159$.

2. In a certain code language, if 'PLANT' is coded as 'QMBOU', how will 'FLOWER' be coded in that same language?

(A) GMPXFS

(B) GMPXES

(C) GMQWFS

(D) HNPYFT

Answer: (A) GMPXFS.

Explanation: Each letter is shifted forward by $+1$ in alphabetical order ($\text{P}\to\text{Q}, \text{L}\to\text{M}, \text{A}\to\text{B}$, etc.). For FLOWER: $\text{F}+1=\text{G}, \text{L}+1=\text{M}, \text{O}+1=\text{P}, \text{W}+1=\text{X}, \text{E}+1=\text{F}, \text{R}+1=\text{S}$.

3. Choose the odd one out from the following group:

(A) Copper

(B) Aluminium

(C) Silver

(D) Silicon

Answer: (D) Silicon.

Explanation: Copper, aluminium, and silver are typical metallic conductors; silicon is a metalloid/semiconductor.

4. A boy walks 12 meters North, turns right and walks 5 meters. How far is he from his starting point?

(A) 17 meters

(B) 13 meters

(C) 7 meters

(D) 15 meters

Answer: (B) 13 meters.

Explanation: By the Pythagorean theorem: $\text{Distance} = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{144 + 25} = \sqrt{169} = 13\text{ m}$.

5. Pointing to a photograph of a woman, Rahul said, "She is the only daughter of my father's mother." How is the woman related to Rahul?

(A) Mother

(B) Sister

(C) Paternal Aunt (Bua)

(D) Grandmother

Answer: (C) Paternal Aunt (Bua).

Explanation: Rahul's father's mother is his paternal grandmother. The only daughter of his grandmother is his father's sister (Rahul's paternal aunt).

6. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship between: Animals, Herbivores, and Tigers?

(A) Three mutually disjoint circles

(B) A large circle containing two separate disjoint inner circles

(C) Two intersecting circles inside a third circle

(D) A large circle enclosing an inner circle, which encloses another circle

Answer: (B) A large circle containing two separate disjoint inner circles.

Explanation: Both Herbivores and Tigers belong to the category "Animals", but Tigers are strictly carnivores and share no overlap with herbivores.

7. In a row of 37 students facing North, Ananya is 14th from the left end. What is her rank from the right end?

(A) 23rd

(B) 24th

(C) 25th

(D) 22nd

Answer: (B) 24th.

Explanation: $\text{Right Rank} = (\text{Total Students} - \text{Left Rank}) + 1 = (37 - 14) + 1 = 23 + 1 = 24\text{th}$.

8. If '$+$' means '$\div$', '$-$' means '$\times$', '$\times$' means '$+$', and '$\div$' means '$-$', then evaluate:

$$20 - 4 + 8 \times 6 \div 3$$
(A) 13

(B) 17

(C) 11

(D) 19

Answer: (A) 13.

Explanation: Replacing operators: $20 \times 4 \div 8 + 6 - 3$.

Applying BODMAS: $20 \times (4/8) + 6 - 3 = (20 \times 0.5) + 6 - 3 = 10 + 6 - 3 = 13$.

9. Which letter replaces the question mark in the sequence: B, E, I, N, T, ___?

(A) Y

(B) Z

(C) A

(D) X

Answer: (C) A.

Explanation: Alphabet positions: $\text{B}(2) \xrightarrow{+3} \text{E}(5) \xrightarrow{+4} \text{I}(9) \xrightarrow{+5} \text{N}(14) \xrightarrow{+6} \text{T}(20) \xrightarrow{+7} 27$. In a 26-letter cycle, the 27th letter wraps around to $\text{A}$.

10. What will be the correct mirror image of the word "SCIENCE" when the mirror is placed vertically to its right?

(A) ƎƆИƎIƆS

(B) SCIENCE

(C) SƆIƎИƆE

Section 2: Core Science (Questions 11–45)
1 Mark Each

11. Which chemical reagent produces a dark blue-black coloration when added to a food item containing starch?

(A) Dilute Copper Sulphate solution

(B) Dilute Iodine solution

(C) Caustic soda solution

(D) Benedict's reagent

Answer: (B) Dilute Iodine solution.

Explanation: Iodine interacts with the amylose coils in starch to form a characteristic charge-transfer polyiodide complex that appears blue-black.

12. Swollen and bleeding gums along with slow wound healing are characteristic deficiency symptoms of which vitamin?

(A) Vitamin A

(B) Vitamin B1

(C) Vitamin C

(D) Vitamin D

Answer: (C) Vitamin C.

Explanation: Scurvy is caused by Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) deficiency, which is essential for collagen synthesis.

13. Which combination of leaf venation and root type is correctly matched?

(A) Parallel venation — Taproot system

(B) Reticulate venation — Fibrous root system

(C) Reticulate venation — Taproot system

(D) Parallel venation — Stilt root system

Answer: (C) Reticulate venation — Taproot system.

Explanation: Dicot plants (e.g., pea, mango) exhibit reticulate venation with taproots. Monocots (e.g., grass, wheat) have parallel venation with fibrous roots.

14. Which part of a flower develops into a fruit after successful fertilization?

(A) Ovule

(B) Stigma

(C) Ovary

(D) Petal

Answer: (C) Ovary.

Explanation: Post-fertilization, the ovary ripens into a fruit, while the fertilized ovules develop into seeds.

15. The loss of excess water as water vapor through the microscopic stomata of leaves is called:

(A) Respiration

(B) Transpiration

(C) Guttation

(D) Evacuation

Answer: (B) Transpiration.

Explanation: Transpiration creates suction pull that helps transport water and dissolved minerals from roots to high foliage.

16. Which type of joint allows movement in only one plane, similar to the opening and closing of a door?

(A) Ball and socket joint

(B) Pivotal joint

(C) Hinge joint

(D) Fixed joint

Answer: (C) Hinge joint.

Explanation: Hinge joints (found in knees and elbows) restrict movement along a single axis (flexion and extension).

17. Earthworms lack bones. Which biological structures allow them to anchor their segmented body to the soil during locomotion?

(A) Flagella

(B) Tentacles

(C) Bristles (Setae)

(D) Webbed feet

Answer: (C) Bristles (Setae).

Explanation: Earthworms use longitudinal and circular muscles along with tiny hair-like projections called setae to grip the ground.

18. Why do desert plants like cacti possess stems that are thick, green, and covered with a waxy layer?

(A) To store food and trap burrowing animals

(B) To carry out photosynthesis while minimizing water loss by transpiration

(C) To deter all microorganisms from attacking root hairs

(D) To help the plant float during sudden flash floods

Answer: (B) To carry out photosynthesis while minimizing water loss by transpiration.

Explanation: Leaves are modified into spines to reduce transpiration surface area; the green fleshy stem handles photosynthesis and stores water.

19. Which respiratory organs are used by dolphins and whales to breathe atmospheric air?

(A) Gills

(B) Moist skin

(C) Blowholes connected to lungs

(D) Spiracles

Answer: (C) Blowholes connected to lungs.

Explanation: Cetaceans are mammals; they lack gills and must surface periodically to breathe through blowholes at the top of their heads into lungs.

20. Which organism is predominantly used in the vermicomposting process to convert organic household waste into nutrient-rich compost?

(A) Roundworms

(B) Redworms (Eisenia fetida)

(C) Flatworms

(D) Leeches

Answer: (B) Redworms (Eisenia fetida).

Explanation: Redworms possess a specialized muscular structure called a gizzard that grinds down decaying plant matter.

21. An object is placed between a light source and a screen. Which type of object will cast a distinct, dark shadow?

(A) Transparent object

(B) Translucent object

(C) Opaque object

(D) Luminous object

Answer: (C) Opaque object.

Explanation: Opaque objects completely block light rays traveling in straight paths, creating an umbra (dark shadow) on the screen.

22. What is the fundamental property of light that accounts for both shadow formation and the operation of a pinhole camera?

(A) Dispersion of light into spectral colors

(B) Rectilinear propagation of light (light travels in straight lines)

(C) Total internal reflection

(D) Polarization of electromagnetic waves

Answer: (B) Rectilinear propagation of light (light travels in straight lines).

Explanation: Because light does not bend around macroscopic corners spontaneously, straight ray trajectories yield both sharp geometric shadows and inverted pinhole images.

23. Which image property is observed when viewing yourself in a clean, vertical plane mirror?

(A) Inverted and diminished

(B) Laterally inverted, virtual, and same size as object

(C) Real, inverted, and magnified

(D) Virtual, erect, and magnified

Answer: (B) Laterally inverted, virtual, and same size as object.

Explanation: Plane mirrors produce upright virtual images with unit magnification ($m = +1$) where left and right appear swapped (lateral inversion).

24. The SI unit for measuring length is:

(A) Centimeter

(B) Kilometer

(C) Meter

(D) Millimeter

Answer: (C) Meter.

Explanation: Under the International System of Units (SI), the meter (m) is the base unit of length.

25. Which type of motion is executed by the blades of a working ceiling fan?

(A) Periodic and rectilinear motion

(B) Circular / Rotational motion

(C) Non-periodic oscillatory motion

(D) Pure rectilinear motion

Answer: (B) Circular / Rotational motion.

Explanation: Points on the blades travel along circular trajectories centered on the fixed central axis of the rotor.

26. Which of the following motions is strictly periodic?

(A) A car taking a turn along a mountain road

(B) The swinging bob of an undisturbed grandfather pendulum

(C) A flying butterfly in a garden

(D) An apple falling from a branch

Answer: (B) The swinging bob of an undisturbed grandfather pendulum.

Explanation: Periodic motion repeats its path over equal, precise intervals of time.

27. In an electric cell, electrical energy is produced from stored:

(A) Thermal energy

(B) Chemical energy

(C) Mechanical energy

(D) Magnetic energy

Answer: (B) Chemical energy.

Explanation: Electrochemical redox reactions between internal electrolytes and electrodes liberate electrons, producing a potential difference.

28. Why are copper wires normally coated with polyvinyl chloride (PVC) or rubber?

(A) PVC is a good electrical conductor

(B) PVC is an electrical insulator that prevents short circuits and electric shocks

(C) PVC decreases the weight of copper metals

(D) PVC chemically reacts with copper to generate electricity

Answer: (B) PVC is an electrical insulator that prevents short circuits and electric shocks.

Explanation: Insulating sheaths prevent direct conductive contact with stray charges or living tissue.

29. Which piece of material will complete an electrical gap and cause an electric bulb in a simple circuit to glow?

(A) A glass rod

(B) A wooden matchstick

(C) An iron nail

(D) A plastic ruler

Answer: (C) An iron nail.

Explanation: Iron is a metallic conductor that allows charge carriers (electrons) to bridge open terminals.

30. Which of the following is a non-magnetic material?

(A) Iron

(B) Cobalt

(C) Nickel

(D) Brass

Answer: (D) Brass.

Explanation: Brass is an alloy of copper and zinc; neither element exhibits ferromagnetism at room temperature.

31. When a bar magnet is suspended freely by a thread from its center of gravity, it naturally aligns along the:

(A) East-West direction

(B) North-South direction

(C) North-East direction

(D) South-West direction

Answer: (B) North-South direction.

Explanation: Earth acts as a dipole magnet; the magnetic north pole of the bar magnet is attracted toward Earth's geomagnetic North (which is magnetically an south-seeking pole).

32. What occurs when the North pole of a permanent magnet is brought close to the North pole of another freely suspended magnet?

(A) Strong magnetic attraction

(B) Magnetic repulsion

(C) Neutralization with zero net force

(D) Generation of electric sparks

Answer: (B) Magnetic repulsion.

Explanation: Like magnetic poles repel each other, whereas unlike poles attract.

33. Which method is most effective for separating wheat grains from dry stalks after harvesting?

(A) Sieving

(B) Threshing

(C) Winnowing

(D) Decantation

Answer: (B) Threshing.

Explanation: Threshing beats dried stalks against hard surfaces (or uses mechanical threshers) to detach the grain seeds.

34. Winnowing relies on which physical difference between the components of a mixture?

(A) Difference in particle color

(B) Difference in boiling points

(C) Difference in weight/density when acted on by blowing wind

(D) Difference in magnetic susceptibility

Answer: (C) Difference in weight/density when acted on by blowing wind.

Explanation: Wind carries lighter husk flakes farther away while heavier grain seeds drop vertically.

35. Which separation sequence correctly isolates clear drinking water from muddy river water?

(A) Evaporation followed by Threshing

(B) Sedimentation, Decantation, followed by Filtration

(C) Magnetic separation followed by Sieving

(D) Winnowing followed by Distillation

Answer: (B) Sedimentation, Decantation, followed by Filtration.

Explanation: Heavy suspended mud particles settle first (sedimentation), the clearer top layer is poured off (decantation), and fine particles are trapped with filter paper (filtration).

36. What is a solution called when no more solute can dissolve in it at a given constant temperature?

(A) Unsaturated solution

(B) Saturated solution

(C) Supersaturated emulsion

(D) Dilute solution

Answer: (B) Saturated solution.

Explanation: At dynamic saturation equilibrium, the rate of dissolution equals the rate of precipitation.

37. Which of the following is a reversible physical change?

(A) Rusting of an iron gate

(B) Burning of a beeswax candle wick

(C) Melting of ice into liquid water

(D) Baking a loaf of bread

Answer: (C) Melting of ice into liquid water.

Explanation: Phase change from solid ice to liquid water involves no change in chemical composition and can be reversed by cooling below 0°C.

38. Which gas constitutes approximately 78% of Earth's atmosphere by volume?

(A) Oxygen

(B) Carbon dioxide

(C) Argon

(D) Nitrogen

Answer: (D) Nitrogen.

Explanation: Clean, dry atmospheric air is composed of ~78.08% Nitrogen, ~20.95% Oxygen, ~0.93% Argon, and ~0.04% Carbon dioxide.

39. Which component of air is essential for respiration in living organisms and supports the combustion of fuels?

(A) Nitrogen

(B) Oxygen

(C) Carbon dioxide

(D) Helium

Answer: (B) Oxygen.

Explanation: Oxygen acts as the terminal electron acceptor in aerobic respiration and sustains exothermic oxidation (combustion).

40. Why do mountaineers carry oxygen cylinders with them when climbing high Himalayan peaks?

(A) High-altitude air is saturated with toxic ozone

(B) Atmospheric air density and oxygen partial pressure drop significantly at high altitudes

(C) High altitudes lack gravity to hold air in place

(D) To protect themselves against wild animals

Answer: (B) Atmospheric air density and oxygen partial pressure drop significantly at high altitudes.

Explanation: As altitude increases, atmospheric pressure drops, making oxygen molecules per breath insufficient for bodily needs (hypoxia).

41. The continuous circulation of water from Earth's surface into the atmosphere and back through evaporation, condensation, and precipitation is known as the:

(A) Nitrogen cycle

(B) Carbon cycle

(C) Water cycle (Hydrological cycle)

(D) Rock cycle

Answer: (C) Water cycle (Hydrological cycle).

Explanation: Solar energy drives surface evaporation and transpiration; vapor condenses into clouds and precipitates as rain, snow, or hail.

42. Which material allows almost all incident light to pass through it, enabling objects on the other side to be seen clearly?

(A) Frosted shower glass

(B) Clear optical glass

(C) Plywood sheet

(D) Cardboard sheet

Answer: (B) Clear optical glass.

Explanation: Transparent materials transmit light with minimal scattering, rendering sharp visibility.

43. Which pair of items will both float when placed on the surface of calm water?

(A) Dry cork and wooden pencil

(B) Iron coin and rubber eraser

(C) Glass marble and steel spoon

(D) Aluminum foil ball and ceramic tile

Answer: (A) Dry cork and wooden pencil.

Explanation: Both cork and most seasoned woods have average densities less than that of liquid water ($1.0\text{ g/cm}^3$).

44. What happens to the solubility of common salt or sugar in water when the temperature of the water is increased?

(A) It decreases dramatically

(B) It remains completely unchanged

(C) It increases

(D) The solution turns into an insoluble gas

Answer: (C) It increases.

Explanation: For most solid solutes like sucrose and salt, dissolution is endothermic; raising temperature increases kinetic energy and solvent capacity.

45. Which of the following is classified as a non-biodegradable waste material?

(A) Banana peels

(B) Discarded newspaper

(C) Polyethylene plastic bag

(D) Cotton fabric scrap

Answer: (C) Polyethylene plastic bag.

Explanation: Synthetic polyethylene lacks chemical bonds accessible to microbial enzyme degradation, persisting for centuries.

3 Marks Each (Total: 15 Marks)

46. An iodine test is performed on four different test tubes containing food extracts:

Tube P: Boiled rice paste 
Tube Q: Diluted egg white solution 
Tube R: Crushed potato extract 
Tube S: Mustard oil droplet 
Which test tubes will exhibit an intense blue-black coloration?

(A) Only P and Q

(B) Only P and R

(C) Only Q and S

(D) Only R and S

Answer: (B) Only P and R.

Explanation: Boiled rice (P) and potato extract (R) are rich in starch, which tests positive (blue-black) with iodine. Egg white contains protein (tested with Biuret reagent), and mustard oil contains lipids (tested with grease/translucent spot test).

47. Analyze the electrical circuit diagram described below:

A circuit contains an electric cell, three identical bulbs ($B_1$, $B_2$, and $B_3$), and two switches ($S_1$ and $S_2$).

$B_1$ and $B_2$ are connected in series along the main loop controlled by switch $S_1$. 
Bulb $B_3$ is in a parallel branch controlled independently by switch $S_2$. 
If switch $S_1$ is opened while switch $S_2$ remains closed, what will be observed?

(A) All three bulbs will glow with equal brightness

(B) Bulbs $B_1$ and $B_2$ will turn OFF, while Bulb $B_3$ remains glowing

(C) Bulb $B_3$ will turn OFF, while $B_1$ and $B_2$ remain glowing

(D) All three bulbs will immediately blow out

Answer: (B) Bulbs $B_1$ and $B_2$ will turn OFF, while Bulb $B_3$ remains glowing.

Explanation: Opening $S_1$ breaks the conductive path for the series branch containing $B_1$ and $B_2$. The parallel loop containing $B_3$ and $S_2$ maintains an unbroken circuit to the cell terminals.

48. A student is provided with a dry mixture containing three substances: Common Salt, Fine Sand, and Iron Filings. Which sequential procedure will separate all three substances in pure states?

(A) Filtration $\rightarrow$ Evaporation $\rightarrow$ Handpicking

(B) Magnetic Separation $\rightarrow$ Dissolution in Water $\rightarrow$ Filtration $\rightarrow$ Evaporation

(C) Winnowing $\rightarrow$ Sedimentation $\rightarrow$ Decantation

(D) Sieving $\rightarrow$ Magnetic Separation $\rightarrow$ Threshing

Answer: (B) Magnetic Separation $\rightarrow$ Dissolution in Water $\rightarrow$ Filtration $\rightarrow$ Evaporation.

Explanation:

Passing a magnet extracts ferromagnetic iron filings. 
Adding water dissolves the soluble salt, leaving insoluble sand. 
Passing the slurry through filter paper retains sand as residue. 
Heating the filtrate evaporates the water, leaving crystallized salt.

50. Consider the following statements regarding the physical motions of everyday objects:

Statement 1: A rolling bowling ball moving along a straight bowling lane executes both rotational motion and rectilinear translation simultaneously. 
Statement 2: The tip of the second hand of a working mechanical wall clock exhibits periodic motion, but does not undergo circular motion. 
Statement 3: The motion of a plucked guitar string is classified as non-periodic random motion. 
Which statement(s) is/are TRUE?

(A) Statements 1 and 2 only

(B) Statement 1 only

(C) Statements 2 and 3 only

(D) Statements 1, 2, and 3

Answer: (B) Statement 1 only.

Explanation:

Statement 1 is correct: A rolling ball spins about its central axis while translating forward linearly. 
Statement 2 is false: The tip moves in a circular path at a constant radius, meaning it executes both circular and periodic motion. 
Statement 3 is false: A vibrating guitar string executes rapid periodic oscillatory motion.
