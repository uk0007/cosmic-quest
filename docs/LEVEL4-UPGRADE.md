# Moonmoss Sanctuary enhancement

Level 4 grows from 6,500 to 17,000 world pixels. Seven question gates, three diamonds, the wizard guide, moss platforms and living-grove assets remain.

Seven extended routes add moss monoliths, flower/fern clusters, moving leaf platforms and cutter pillars mounted on moss columns. Early routes introduce thorn bypasses, vertical lifts and energy-shot gaps. The final three routes combine green slime patrols, flying attackers, orange armored slimes, sweeping cutters and repeated cannon fire.

Totals: 16 enemies, 10 vertical cutters, three sweeping cutters, six cannons, nine energy-shot gaps and nine moving platforms. The original three collapsing platforms remain. Cannon warning lights precede shots; projectile lanes end before question checkpoints.

Three timed encounters precede Gates 5–7: Spore Bridge (2.1-second opening), Thornvine Lock (1.9 seconds), and Sanctuary Heart (1.7 seconds). They have solid staging banks, landing shelves, countdown signs and energy-shot suppression during openings. Wind is kept out of the new timed waiting areas.

Levels 1–3 retain their existing layouts. Shared trap rendering selects moss columns for Level 4. Validation includes all-level static checkpoint/layout checks, a full touch-control playthrough with combat active, and isolated timed-jump fixtures. Results and screenshots are stored under `artifacts/level4-upgrade/`. Software-rendered results do not certify physical-phone frame rates.

The full touch playthrough completed all seven gates and three diamonds with zero falls and 100% final energy, recording moving-platform contact. The last level correctly has no Next Level button. Software-rendered average was 12.4 FPS. A wind zone over the first timed staging area was removed after this playthrough started; the isolated fixtures exercise that final configuration.
