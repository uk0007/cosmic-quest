# Late Level 1 timing trials

Two mandatory timed crossings precede the final two question gates. The level remains 14,600 pixels long with seven question gates.

- **Before Gate 6:** Rhythm Ravine at x=10,030–10,270. Its cutter barrier stays closed for 3.3 seconds, then retracts for 2.1 seconds.
- **Before Gate 7:** Final Clockwork Crossing at x=13,200–13,500. A wider new gap has a 3.6-second closed phase and a tighter 1.8-second opening.

Tall rotating cutter barriers block both the walking path and a double-jump bypass while closed. Amber countdown signs and pulsing barriers warn the player; cyan “JUMP!” signs mark the safe window. Energy shots inside these ravines are suppressed during that window. Landing shelves and solid waiting banks allow staging before committing. The next gates remain 430 and 460 pixels beyond the gaps.

Trial clocks activate on approach and stop with the existing game/quiz pause. Existing damage and invulnerability rules apply on contact. Changes affect Level 1 only.

Validation: static level/checkpoint checks for all four levels. Dedicated browser fixtures open question gates and remove enemies to isolate the timing challenge, then wait for the opening and cross using ordinary right/jump inputs. Results and screenshots live in `artifacts/timing-trials/`; this is a focused traversal test rather than a full campaign replay.
