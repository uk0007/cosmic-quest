# Level 3 — Stormglass Citadel

A distinct cloud-temple environment replaces the illustrated summit landscape: marble and gold landing surfaces, distant blue towers, drifting cloud banks and rotating celestial rings. Question/vault gates use pale marble frames; the ending arch retains the blue-lit visible dog entry animation. The existing 16,000-pixel route, seven question gates and three diamond vaults remain.

Three new generated enemy skins and two movement behaviors are specific to this level. Spark Hoppers periodically jump during ground patrols. Storm Rays dive and recover during aerial patrols. Aegis Crabs retain two-hit armor with their own shell appearance. Three additional rays raise the enemy count to sixteen. Later levels retain their separate authored enemies.

Validation: all campaign layouts pass. Isolated browser checks verify hopping, ray motion, armor damage with preserved custom skin, and gate/door texture selection. A Level 3 ending fixture confirms the dog remains visible during entry and reaches victory. Full combat balance has not been re-certified.
