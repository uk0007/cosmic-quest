# Starlight Summit upgrade

Level 3 now spans 16,000 world pixels, with seven question gates and three diamonds. Seven extended open-air routes use mountain spires, rune pillars, cairns and drifting clouds beneath the cliffs.

- Nine vertical cutters and two sweeping cutters.
- Four repeating cannons, firing at 1.1/1.4-second intervals with visible warning lights; projectile speed is 350 pixels/second.
- Thirteen enemies, including six added ground/flying/armored attackers in the last two routes.
- Eight energy-shot gaps and nine moving platforms, retaining the original collapsing rocks and geysers.
- Two additional gentle wind zones over the raised routes in sections 3 and 5.
- Before Gates 6 and 7, timed skybridge barriers open for 1.9 and 1.65 seconds. Shots within those gaps pause during the advertised opening. Wide staging and recovery ground separates the encounters from question gates.

This is a modest step up from Level 2 through slightly wider crossings, quicker platform/cannon cycles and shorter final openings; damage remains unchanged. Level 4 is unchanged.

Static checks verify all four levels, seven gates, collectible counts, cavern/summit exits and safe checkpoints. Browser evidence is under `artifacts/level3-upgrade/`. The full touch playthrough retains combat; isolated timing fixtures remove enemies and open gates to check the two jump windows. Software-rendered checks do not certify physical-phone frame rates.

The full touch playthrough completed seven gates and three diamonds with zero falls and 100% final energy. Moving-platform contact was recorded. Software-rendered average was 17.6 FPS; these results establish functional traversal rather than a physical-device performance target. A final decorative cloud extension was made after starting that playthrough and is included in the timing-fixture build.
