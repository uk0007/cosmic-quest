Section 1: General Awareness (Questions 1–30)
1 Mark Each

1. Which is the largest individual flower in the world, known for emitting a strong odor of decaying meat to attract pollinating flies?

(A) Titan Arum

(B) Rafflesia arnoldii

(C) Hydnora africana

(D) Nelumbo nucifera

Answer: (B) Rafflesia arnoldii.

Explanation: Native to the rainforests of Southeast Asia, Rafflesia arnoldii produces flowers up to 1 meter in diameter and has no leaves, roots, or stems.

2. Lichens represent a mutually beneficial symbiotic relationship between which two organisms?

(A) Bacteria and Virus

(B) Fungus and Alga (or Cyanobacterium)

(C) Protozoa and Fern

(D) Moss and Plant Root

Answer: (B) Fungus and Alga (or Cyanobacterium).

Explanation: The alga synthesizes food via photosynthesis, while the fungal partner provides structural shelter, water, and minerals.

3. Which bird is the only known avian species capable of flying backward and upside down?

(A) Kingfisher

(B) Hummingbird

(C) Swift

(D) Woodpecker

Answer: (B) Hummingbird.

Explanation: Hummingbirds have ball-and-socket shoulder joints that allow their wings to rotate 180 degrees in a figure-eight pattern.

4. What is the process called when an animal blends into its surroundings to hide from predators or prey?

(A) Hibernation

(B) Camouflage

(C) Migration

(D) Aestivation

Answer: (B) Camouflage.

Explanation: Camouflage uses color patterns, shapes, or textures to blend an organism into its natural environment.

5. Which is the deepest oceanic trench on Earth?

(A) Puerto Rico Trench

(B) Java Trench

(C) Mariana Trench

(D) Romanche Trench

Answer: (C) Mariana Trench.

Explanation: Located in the western Pacific Ocean, its deepest point (Challenger Deep) plunges to approximately 11,000 meters below sea level.

6. The "Ring of Fire", known for frequent earthquakes and active volcanoes, encircles the basin of which ocean?

(A) Atlantic Ocean

(B) Indian Ocean

(C) Arctic Ocean

(D) Pacific Ocean

Answer: (D) Pacific Ocean.

Explanation: Around 75% of Earth’s active and dormant volcanoes lie along the edges of tectonic plates in the Pacific basin.

7. Which greenhouse gas is released in substantial amounts from flooded paddy fields and digestive processes of cattle?

(A) Nitrous oxide

(B) Methane

(C) Carbon monoxide

(D) Sulfur dioxide

Answer: (B) Methane.

Explanation: Methanogenic bacteria in waterlogged rice soils and ruminant livestock digestive tracts produce significant amounts of methane ($\text{CH}_4$).

8. What is the hardest naturally occurring mineral substance on Earth?

(A) Quartz

(B) Topaz

(C) Diamond

(D) Corundum

Explanation: Diamond ranks at the maximum value of 10 on the Mohs hardness scale due to its tetrahedral covalent carbon lattice.

11. Why does Mars appear reddish in night sky observations?

(A) Presence of molten red lava across the surface

(B) High abundance of iron oxide (rust) in its regolith and dust

(C) Reflection of infrared sunlight by thick methane clouds

(D) Radiation from its twin moons

Answer: (B) High abundance of iron oxide (rust) in its regolith and dust.

Explanation: Fine surface dust rich in oxidized iron absorbs blue and green wavelengths, giving Mars its characteristic reddish tint.

29. Logical Reasoning: Look at the sequence: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ___? Which number comes next?

(A) 36

(B) 40

(C) 42

(D) 44

Answer: (C) 42.

Explanation: The differences between terms increase by $+2$: $+4, +6, +8, +10, +12$. Thus, $30 + 12 = 42$ (or $n \times (n+1)$ where $6 \times 7 = 42$).

30. Reasoning / Clock Angle: What is the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at exactly 6:00?

(A) 90°

(B) 120°

(C) 180°

(D) 360°

Answer: (C) 180°.

Explanation: At 6:00, the minute hand points to 12 and the hour hand points directly opposite to 6, forming a straight line ($180^\circ$).

42. What is the recommended first-aid step for a minor thermal burn on a finger?

(A) Rub ice cubes directly over the blistered skin

(B) Hold the burned area under cool running water for 10 to 15 minutes

(C) Apply butter or oil immediately

(D) Puncture the fluid-filled blister with a needle

Answer: (B) Hold the burned area under cool running water for 10 to 15 minutes.

49. Quantitative / Calendar Reasoning: If January 1 of a leap year falls on a Sunday, on which day of the week will December 31 of that same year fall?

(A) Sunday

(B) Monday

(C) Tuesday

(D) Saturday

Answer: (B) Monday.

Explanation: A leap year contains 366 days ($52\text{ weeks} + 2\text{ odd days}$). Therefore, the 365th day falls on Sunday, and the 366th day (December 31) falls on the next day, Monday. (A regular year ends on the same weekday it begins; a leap year ends on the following weekday).
