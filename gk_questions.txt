(A) Sunday

(B) Monday

(C) Tuesday

(D) Saturday

Answer: (B) Monday.

Explanation: A leap year contains 366 days (52 weeks + 2 odd days). Therefore, the 365th day falls on Sunday, and the 366th day (December 31) falls on the next day, Monday. (A regular year ends on the same weekday it begins; a leap year ends on the following weekday).
