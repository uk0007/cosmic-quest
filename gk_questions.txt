This 50-question mock bank mirrors the standard Class 6 International General Knowledge Olympiad (IGKO) format, divided across General Awareness, Current Affairs, Life Skills, and the higher-order Achievers Section.

Section 1: General Awareness (Questions 1–30)
1. Which carnivorous plant catches insects by snapping its modified leaf lobes shut in less than a second?

(A) Pitcher plant

(B) Venus flytrap

(C) Sundew

(D) Rafflesia

Answer: (B) Venus flytrap. Rapid turgor pressure changes trigger the sensitive trigger hairs to snap shut.

2. Which flightless bird native to New Zealand has nostrils located at the tip of its long bill to sniff out insects underground?

(A) Emu

(B) Ostrich

(C) Kiwi

(D) Penguin

Answer: (C) Kiwi. It is one of the few birds that relies heavily on smell rather than sight to hunt.

3. The periodic shedding of outer skin, feathers, or exoskeletons to facilitate new growth is known as:

(A) Hibernation

(B) Moulting

(C) Camouflage

(D) Metamorphosis

Answer: (B) Moulting. Seen commonly in reptiles, insects, and birds.

4. Plants that grow on other trees purely for physical support without drawing nutrients or causing harm are called:

(A) Parasites

(B) Epiphytes

(C) Saprophytes

(D) Xerophytes

Answer: (B) Epiphytes. Many tropical orchids and ferns are classic examples.

5. Which layer of Earth's atmosphere contains the vital ozone layer that absorbs harmful UV rays?

(A) Troposphere

(B) Stratosphere

(C) Mesosphere

(D) Thermosphere

Answer: (B) Stratosphere. It sits directly above the troposphere.

6. What is the line dividing the illuminated day side from the dark night side of a planetary body called?

(A) Prime Meridian

(B) Terminator line

(C) Horizon

(D) Tropic of Cancer

Answer: (B) Terminator line. Also known as the "twilight zone."

7. Which is the world's largest hot desert?

(A) Gobi Desert

(B) Kalahari Desert

(C) Sahara Desert

(D) Thar Desert

Answer: (C) Sahara Desert. (Antarctica is the largest desert overall, but Sahara is the largest hot desert).

8. What term describes the progressive increase in the concentration of toxins (like microplastics or heavy metals) at higher levels of the food chain?

(A) Eutrophication

(B) Biomagnification

(C) Desertification

(D) Decomposition

Answer: (B) Biomagnification. Apex predators accumulate the highest concentrations.

9. Which planet in our solar system is known as the "Morning Star" or "Evening Star" due to its exceptional brightness?

(A) Mars

(B) Venus

(C) Jupiter

(D) Mercury

Answer: (B) Venus. Its thick cloud cover reflects sunlight strongly (high albedo).

10. Which mineral is indispensable for the production of hemoglobin in human red blood cells?

29. Logical Reasoning: If South-West becomes North, and North-West becomes East, what will West become?

(A) North-East

(B) South-East

(C) South

(D) North

Answer: (A) North-East.

Explanation: The rotation is 135° clockwise (SW + 135° = North; NW + 135° = East). Rotating West (270°) by 135° clockwise yields 45°, which is North-East.

30. Number Series: Find the missing term: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ___?

(A) 95

(B) 125

(C) 127

(D) 129

Answer: (C) 127.

Explanation: Each term follows $(n \times 2) + 1$, or successive additions of double increments ($+4, +8, +16, +32, +64$). $63 + 64 = 127$.

Section 3: Life Skills & Values (Questions 41–45)
41. During a group project, a member proposes a concept completely different from yours. What is the most constructive action?

(A) Overrule them immediately and insist on your approach

(B) Listen patiently, evaluate the merits of their idea, and find a way to combine both perspectives

(C) Withdraw from the group and complain to the teacher

(D) Ignore the member and complete the project alone

Answer: (B). Demonstrates collaborative problem solving, active listening, and open-mindedness.

42. What is "phishing" in the context of cyber safety?

(A) Playing virtual fishing games online

(B) Deceptive messages or emails designed to steal passwords, financial details, or personal data

(C) Updating your computer's firewall software

(D) Browsing educational websites with parent supervision

Answer: (B). Phishing attempts mimic trusted services to trick users.

43. If an alarming, unverified message about an impending emergency is forwarded in a chat group, what is the right digital response?

(A) Immediately forward it to all your contacts to warn them

(B) Cross-check with credible news outlets or official school authorities before sharing

(C) Retweet and post it on public forums

(D) Panic and leave all groups

Answer: (B). Stopping the spread of fake news is a core digital literacy skill.

44. What is the healthiest way to manage acute exam stress and cognitive fatigue?

(A) Pulling all-nighters and skipping breakfast

(B) Planning study intervals with the Pomodoro technique, sleeping 8 hours, and practicing deep breathing

(C) Quitting revision entirely

(D) Relying on energy drinks and sugary snacks

Answer: (B). Structured rest and predictable scheduling protect memory consolidation and reduce anxiety.

45. What is the core definition of "empathy"?

(A) Feeling pity from a superior position

(B) Understanding and sharing the feelings and perspectives of another person

(C) Agreeing with someone even when they are wrong

(D) Offering advice before hearing someone out

Answer: (B). Empathy involves emotionally and cognitively understanding another's lived experience.
