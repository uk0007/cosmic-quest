Section 1: Logical Reasoning (Questions 1–10)
1 Mark Each

1. Find the next number in the given pattern: 5, 10, 15, 20, ___?

(A) 22

(B) 25

(C) 30

(D) 35

Answer: (B) 25.

Explanation: The pattern adds $5$ to each term ($20 + 5 = 25$).

2. Which letter comes next in the alphabetical series: A, C, E, G, ___?

(A) H

(B) I

(C) J

(D) K

Answer: (B) I.

Explanation: Every alternate letter is skipped ($\text{A} \xrightarrow{+2} \text{C} \xrightarrow{+2} \text{E} \xrightarrow{+2} \text{G} \xrightarrow{+2} \text{I}$).

3. Choose the odd one out from the options:

(A) Rose

(B) Jasmine

(C) Lily

(D) Apple

Answer: (D) Apple.

Explanation: Rose, Jasmine, and Lily are flowers; Apple is a fruit.

4. If 'CAT' is coded as 'DBU' (+1 letter forward), how will 'DOG' be coded?

(A) EPH

(B) FPI

(C) EOF

(D) DPH

Answer: (A) EPH.

Explanation: Move each letter forward by one step: $\text{D} \to \text{E}$, $\text{O} \to \text{P}$, $\text{G} \to \text{H}$.

5. Complete the analogy: Eye : See :: Ear : ___?

(A) Smell

(B) Hear

(C) Taste

(D) Touch

Answer: (B) Hear.

Explanation: Eyes are the organs for seeing; ears are the organs for hearing.

6. If you are facing North and take a sharp turn to your right, which direction are you facing now?

(A) South

(B) West

(C) East

(D) North-West

Answer: (C) East.

Explanation: Turning 90° right from North points toward East.

7. How is your father's brother related to you?

(A) Grandfather

(B) Brother

(C) Uncle

(D) Cousin

Answer: (C) Uncle.

Explanation: A father's brother is one's paternal uncle.

8. Which word CANNOT be formed using the letters of the word "TEACHER"?

(A) TRICK

(B) REACH

(C) CHEAT

(D) HEAR

Answer: (A) TRICK.

Explanation: The word "TRICK" cannot be formed because the letters 'I' and 'K' are not present in "TEACHER". All other options (REACH, CHEAT, HEAR) can be formed using the letters of "TEACHER".

9. At 3:00 o'clock, what angle is formed between the hour hand and the minute hand?

(A) Acute angle (less than 90°)

(B) Right angle (90°)

(C) Obtuse angle (more than 90°)

(D) Straight angle (180°)

Answer: (B) Right angle (90°).

Explanation: The minute hand is at 12 and the hour hand is at 3, making a 90° right angle.

10. Count the total number of triangles in a square divided into four equal parts by two diagonals:

(A) 4

(B) 6

(C) 8

(D) 10

Answer: (C) 8.

Explanation: There are 4 small triangles and 4 larger composite triangles formed by pairs of smaller ones ($4 + 4 = 8$).

Section 2: Core Science (Questions 11–45)
1 Mark Each

11. Which part of the plant absorbs water and mineral salts from the soil?

(A) Stem

(B) Leaf

(C) Root

(D) Flower

Answer: (C) Root.

Explanation: Roots anchor the plant and absorb moisture and essential minerals from the soil.

12. The process by which green leaves prepare food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide is called:

(A) Respiration

(B) Photosynthesis

(C) Transpiration

(D) Germination

Answer: (B) Photosynthesis.

Explanation: Chlorophyll in leaves traps sunlight to produce glucose through photosynthesis.

13. Night blindness is caused by the deficiency of which vitamin?

(A) Vitamin A

(B) Vitamin B

(C) Vitamin C

(D) Vitamin D

Answer: (A) Vitamin A.

Explanation: Lack of Vitamin A impairs night vision and damages eye health.

14. Which nutrient acts as the primary "bodybuilder" to help children grow and repair injured tissues?

(A) Carbohydrates

(B) Fats

(C) Proteins

(D) Roughage

Answer: (C) Proteins.

Explanation: Proteins are essential building blocks for muscle growth, tissue repair, and cell maintenance.

15. Weak, soft, and bent bones in young children (Rickets) are caused by a lack of:

(A) Vitamin D

(B) Vitamin C

(C) Vitamin K

(D) Iron

Answer: (A) Vitamin D.

Explanation: Vitamin D and calcium are required for bone hardening; a deficiency results in rickets.

16. Animals that eat only plants or plant parts are called:

(A) Carnivores

(B) Herbivores

(C) Omnivores

(D) Scavengers

Answer: (B) Herbivores.

Explanation: Herbivores (like cows, deer, and rabbits) feed strictly on plant matter.

17. Which of the following animals is an omnivore?

(A) Cow

(B) Tiger

(C) Crow

(D) Lion

Answer: (C) Crow.

Explanation: Crows eat both grains/seeds and small insects or meat scraps.

18. The loss of excess water as water vapor from leaf stomata into the air is known as:

(A) Evaporation

(B) Condensation

(C) Transpiration

(D) Precipitation

Answer: (C) Transpiration.

Explanation: Transpiration cools the plant and creates suction to pull water upward from the roots.

19. Which type of root system does a grass plant have?

(A) Taproot system

(B) Fibrous root system

(C) Stilt root system

(D) Aerial root system

Answer: (B) Fibrous root system.

Explanation: Grasses have a bunch of similar, thin roots originating together, typical of monocot plants.

20. The elbow and knee joints allow bending movement in only one direction. They are examples of:

(A) Ball and socket joints

(B) Hinge joints

(C) Pivotal joints

(D) Fixed joints

Answer: (B) Hinge joints.

Explanation: Like the hinge of a door, a hinge joint moves strictly backward and forward in one plane.

21. Fish breathe underwater using which specialized organ?

(A) Lungs

(B) Gills

(C) Skin

(D) Fins

Answer: (B) Gills.

Explanation: Gills absorb dissolved oxygen from the water as it passes through them.

22. Which adaptation helps camels survive long periods without water in hot deserts?

(A) Thick fur all over their eyes

(B) Storing fat in their hump and passing very little urine

(C) Webbed feet for paddling

(D) Moist skin for breathing

Answer: (B) Storing fat in their hump and passing very little urine.

Explanation: These features conserve moisture and provide metabolic energy over long dry spells.

23. An object that does NOT allow any light to pass through it is described as:

(A) Transparent

(B) Translucent

(C) Opaque

(D) Luminous

Answer: (C) Opaque.

Explanation: Opaque objects (like wood, brick, or cardboard) block light completely, forming shadows.

24. Which of the following is a natural source of light?

(A) Torch bulb

(B) Tube light

(C) The Sun

(D) Burning candle

Answer: (C) The Sun.

Explanation: The Sun produces its own natural light, while torches and candles are artificial sources.

25. What is the standard SI unit of length?

(A) Centimeter

(B) Meter

(C) Kilometer

(D) Millimeter

Answer: (B) Meter.

Explanation: Under the International System of Units (SI), the meter (m) is the standard base unit for length.

26. The motion of a stone dropped from the top of a terrace straight down to the ground is:

(A) Circular motion

(B) Rectilinear (straight-line) motion

(C) Periodic motion

(D) Rotational motion

Answer: (B) Rectilinear (straight-line) motion.

Explanation: Motion along a straight track or vertical drop is rectilinear.

27. The back-and-forth movement of a swing repeats at regular intervals. This motion is:

(A) Rectilinear motion

(B) Periodic motion

(C) Random motion

(D) Circular motion

Answer: (B) Periodic motion.

Explanation: Motion that repeats itself after regular intervals of time is periodic.

28. An electric cell converts which form of energy into electrical energy?

(A) Heat energy

(B) Chemical energy

(C) Light energy

(D) Sound energy

Answer: (B) Chemical energy.

Explanation: Chemicals stored inside the battery react to produce an electric current.

29. Which of the following is a good electrical conductor?

(A) Plastic comb

(B) Wooden toothpick

(C) Iron nail

(D) Rubber band

Answer: (C) Iron nail.

Explanation: Metals like iron allow electric current to flow through them easily.

30. Why are electric wires covered with plastic sheaths?

(A) Plastic makes wires look attractive

(B) Plastic is an insulator that protects us from electric shocks

(C) Plastic makes electricity flow faster

(D) Plastic cools down the copper wire

Answer: (B) Plastic is an insulator that protects us from electric shocks.

Explanation: Plastic does not conduct electricity and safely shields the bare conductive metal wires.

31. A magnet always has how many poles?

(A) One

(B) Two (North and South)

(C) Three

(D) Four

Answer: (B) Two (North and South).

Explanation: Every magnet, regardless of size or shape, possesses a North pole and a South pole.

32. What happens when the North pole of one magnet is brought near the North pole of another?

(A) They attract each other

(B) They repel (push away from) each other

(C) Nothing happens

(D) They melt together

Answer: (B) They repel (push away from) each other.

Explanation: Like magnetic poles repel, while opposite poles (North and South) attract.

33. Which of the following is an example of a reversible change?

(A) Burning of a paper sheet

(B) Melting of ice into water

(C) Baking a raw cake

(D) Cooking an egg

Answer: (B) Melting of ice into water.

Explanation: Water can be refrozen back into ice by cooling; the change can be easily reversed.

34. Handpicking is the best method to separate:

(A) Salt dissolved in clear water

(B) Small pebbles from a bowl of rice grains

(C) Oil mixed with water

(D) Fine tea dust from water

Answer: (B) Small pebbles from a bowl of rice grains.

Explanation: Handpicking works when impurities are visible, distinct in shape/size, and present in small amounts.

35. Farmers separate grain seeds from dry harvested stalks using which process?

(A) Winnowing

(B) Threshing

(C) Sieving

(D) Filtration

Answer: (B) Threshing.

Explanation: Beating stalks on a hard surface or using machines to loosen seeds is threshing.

36. Common salt is obtained from shallow seawater pools mainly by:

(A) Condensation

(B) Evaporation

(C) Sieving

(D) Decantation

Answer: (B) Evaporation.

Explanation: The sun's heat evaporates the water liquid, leaving solid crystals of salt behind.

37. When no more sugar can dissolve in a glass of water at room temperature, the solution is:

(A) Saturated

(B) Unsaturated

(C) Insoluble

(D) Dilute

Answer: (A) Saturated.

Explanation: A saturated solution has dissolved the maximum solute possible at that specific temperature.

38. Which gas is present in the largest quantity in Earth's atmosphere?

(A) Oxygen

(B) Carbon dioxide

(C) Nitrogen

(D) Water vapor

Answer: (C) Nitrogen.

Explanation: Nitrogen makes up about 78% of the air by volume.

39. Which gas in the air is essential for burning (combustion) and animal breathing?

(A) Nitrogen

(B) Oxygen

(C) Carbon dioxide

(D) Argon

Answer: (B) Oxygen.

Explanation: Oxygen supports respiration in living cells and sustains flame combustion.

40. An example of a transparent object is:

(A) Clean glass window

(B) Oiled paper

(C) Cardboard box

(D) Wooden door

Answer: (A) Clean glass window.

Explanation: Clean clear glass allows light to pass directly through, allowing objects behind it to be seen clearly.

41. Which of the following items will float on top of water?

(A) Iron coin

(B) Dry wooden cork

(C) Glass marble

(D) Heavy stone

Answer: (B) Dry wooden cork.

Explanation: Cork is lighter (less dense) than water, so it floats on the surface.

42. The skull bones in our head (except the lower jaw) cannot move because they are joined by:

(A) Hinge joints

(B) Fixed joints

(C) Ball and socket joints

(D) Gliding joints

Answer: (B) Fixed joints.

Explanation: Cranial bones are interlocked by fixed, immovable joints to protect the brain.

43. Redworms used in vermicomposting help convert kitchen vegetable scraps into:

(A) Plastic beads

(B) Organic compost/manure

(C) Toxic gases

(D) Ceramic powder

Answer: (B) Organic compost/manure.

Explanation: Redworms break down organic kitchen waste into nutrient-rich vermicompost.

44. Which of the following is a non-biodegradable waste material?

(A) Banana peel

(B) Apple core

(C) Plastic carry bag

(D) Dried tree leaves

Answer: (C) Plastic carry bag.

Explanation: Plastics do not break down or rot naturally through microorganism activity.

45. Water turns into water vapor when heated. This process is called:

(A) Condensation

(B) Evaporation

(C) Freezing

(D) Melting

Answer: (B) Evaporation.

Explanation: Evaporation is the conversion of liquid water into gaseous water vapor upon warming.

Section 3: Achievers Section (HOTS) (Questions 46–50)
3 Marks Each (Total: 15 Marks)

46. A student tests food samples with a few drops of dilute Iodine solution. Which of the following will turn blue-black?

(A) Boiled potato slice

(B) A slice of butter

(C) Boiled egg white

(D) Pure cooking oil

Answer: (A) Boiled potato slice.

Explanation: Potatoes are rich in starch; starch produces a distinctive blue-black color when exposed to iodine solution.

47. Look at the simple circuit: A battery, an electric bulb, and two connection wires. What will happen if the switch is kept OPEN?

(A) The bulb will glow brighter

(B) The bulb will NOT glow because the circuit is broken

(C) The battery will heat up and explode

(D) The bulb will glow and change colors

Answer: (B) The bulb will NOT glow because the circuit is broken.

Explanation: An open switch creates a gap, preventing electric current from completing the loop to light the bulb.

48. Match the item in Column I with its property in Column II:

Column I
Column II
(p) Clear water
1. Opaque
(q) Tracing paper
2. Transparent
(r) Brick wall
3. Translucent
(A) p-2, q-3, r-1

(B) p-1, q-2, r-3

(C) p-3, q-1, r-2

(D) p-2, q-1, r-3

Answer: (A) p-2, q-3, r-1.

Explanation: Clear water lets light pass completely (transparent); tracing paper lets partial light through (translucent); a brick wall blocks light completely (opaque).

49. A mixture contains iron pins, sand, and water. What is the easiest order to separate all three?

(A) Evaporation $\to$ Filtration

(B) Use a magnet to remove pins $\to$ Filter the water to remove sand

(C) Handpick sand $\to$ Boil the iron pins

(D) Winnowing $\to$ Sieving

Answer: (B) Use a magnet to remove pins $\to$ Filter the water to remove sand.

Explanation: A magnet pulls out the magnetic iron pins first; pouring the mixture through filter paper then isolates the insoluble sand from water.

50. When a bar magnet is suspended freely by a thin cotton thread, in which direction does it naturally rest?

(A) East-West direction

(B) North-South direction

(C) Up-Down vertical direction

(D) Any random direction

Answer: (B) North-South direction.

Explanation: Earth behaves like a giant magnet; a freely hanging magnet always lines up with the Earth's North-South magnetic poles.
