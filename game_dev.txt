==================================================
1. HIGH-LEVEL GAME CONCEPT
==================================================

The player controls a DOG character in a colorful side-scrolling adventure world.

The dog:

- walks/runs forward
- moves left/right
- jumps over obstacles
- collects bones
- collects energy/crystals
- avoids hazards
- encounters enemies
- eventually gains an attack/projectile ability
- reaches locked Knowledge Gates periodically
- must answer educational MCQs to unlock those gates
- gains energy and powers for correct answers
- loses energy for wrong answers or timeout
- becomes stronger through consecutive correct answers

The overall gameplay loop should feel like:

RUN / EXPLORE
→ JUMP / AVOID OBSTACLES
→ COLLECT ITEMS
→ ENCOUNTER ENEMIES
→ REACH KNOWLEDGE GATE
→ GAME PAUSES
→ QUESTION APPEARS
→ ANSWER
→ REWARD / PENALTY
→ GATE OPENS
→ CONTINUE ADVENTURE

The experience should feel like a real game first and an educational quiz naturally integrated into the adventure.

Do NOT make it look like a quiz page with a character moving in the background.
